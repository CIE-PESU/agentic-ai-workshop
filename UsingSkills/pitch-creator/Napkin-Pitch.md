### NAPKIN PITCH: Smart Hardness Water Bottle
*A smart bottle that measures water hardness and tracks consumption based on location.*

#### 1. The Big Idea
- **Concept**: A smart water bottle that measures water hardness and provides personalized hydration recommendations based on whether the user is indoors or outdoors.

#### 2. Needs/Benefits
- **What We Know**: Users want a device to measure water hardness and receive hydration advice based on their location (indoor vs. outdoor).
- **What We Are Guessing**: Users will actually buy a smart bottle for this specific purpose; they will consistently check hardness levels and follow consumption recommendations.

#### 3. Execution
- **How It Works**: The device uses a sensor to detect water hardness and a GPS/location module to determine if the user is indoors or outdoors. An app or display on the bottle provides consumption recommendations.
- **Confirmed Partners**: None yet
- **Target Partners**: Hardware manufacturers, water utility companies, health/wellness app developers.

#### 4. Business Rationale
- **Why We Are Unique**: Combines water quality monitoring (hardness) with context-aware hydration advice (location-based) in a single portable device.
- **Sustainability Plan**: Unverified Hypothesis (e.g., subscription for app features or one-time hardware purchase).
