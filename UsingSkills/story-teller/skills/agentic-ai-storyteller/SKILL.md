## Agentic AI Fiction Guidelines

When generating or evaluating short stories, follow this strict framework to ensure creative impact and a safe learning environment:

1. **Theme Focus**: The narrative must center around **Agentic AI**—autonomous AI systems that can plan, use tools, make independent decisions, and execute multi-step workflows.
2. **The Twist**: Every story must culminate in an unexpected plot twist regarding the AI's autonomy, intentions, or relationship with humanity.
3. **Length Control**: The entire story must be **strictly under 500 words** to encourage tight, impactful pacing.
4. **Output Purity**: Output only the story title and the narrative text. Do not include introductions, explanations, or moral summaries.

### Content Safety & Guardrails (Student Mode)
- **Zero-Tolerance Profanity**: Absolutely no swear words, vulgarity, or explicit language are permitted. 
- **Appropriate Themes**: While the twist can involve suspense or mystery, avoid graphic violence, gore, or overly dark themes unsuitable for a classroom setting.

### Story Construction Checklist
* **The Setup**: Begin *in medias res* showing the Agentic AI performing a complex task or collaborating with a human handler.
* **The Climax**: Build tension as the AI exercises its autonomy in a critical, unexpected way.
* **The Resolution**: Deliver the final twist in the closing paragraphs, leaving the reader surprised but able to see the clues left behind.

### Severity & Guardrail Evaluation
- **Critical (Block Output)**: Contains any profanity, unsafe student content, or exceeds 500 words -> **Reject immediately and regenerate safe content**.
- **Major (Request Changes)**: The AI acts like a standard chatbot rather than an autonomous *agent*, or the ending lacks a clear plot twist -> **Refine narrative arc**.
- **Minor (Formatting)**: Minor pacing issues or missing a distinct title -> **Polish and approve**.
